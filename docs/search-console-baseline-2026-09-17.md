## Measurement after launch

- Record the actual launch date; it is not yet set.
- Confirm the revised homepage and new pages are crawlable, have correct canonicals, and become indexed.
- Compare equal-length periods, preferably trailing 28-day windows, as data accumulates. Retain the baseline above for context; do not compare its full period directly with a single month as if durations were equal.
- Review impressions and clicks for each landing page and relevant visible non-brand queries. Treat averages cautiously while samples are small.
- Track demo interactions and completed signups separately if analytics instrumentation is available; Search Console alone does not measure them.
- Use three to four months as a review checkpoint for meaningful progress, not a promise of first-page rankings or a specific visitor count. If exposure remains flat, reassess indexing, page usefulness, search intent, competing pages, and legitimate sources of discovery rather than repeatedly swapping keywords.
