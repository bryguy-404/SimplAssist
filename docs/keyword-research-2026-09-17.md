# SimplAssist keyword research — September 17, 2026

Research only. No website changes, commits, or deployment are part of this report.

**Strategy update after discussion and Search Console review:** the user chose to prioritize missed-call text back on the homepage, with separate website-chat and Voice pages. The keyword measurements below are unchanged; the original page allocation below records the earlier $10-homepage preference. The current page plan and verified site baseline are in [the Search Console review](./search-console-baseline-2026-09-17.md).

## Recommendation

Use **chatbot for small business** as the homepage's primary keyword family, with natural supporting language about AI website chat, $10/month pricing, business-specific answers, lead capture, and Google Calendar booking. Suggested customer-facing headline: **An AI chatbot for your small business — $10/month.** Clarify immediately that this is website chat; phone and text features have separate plan requirements.

The strongest researched lower-difficulty opportunity is **missed call text back** on a dedicated feature page: estimated 390 U.S. searches/month and Ahrefs Easy. Prioritize this page after clarifying the homepage. The $10 Chat Only plan does not include this feature, so that page must explain eligible SMS plans and pricing accurately.

Use **appointment booking chatbot** as a smaller supporting opportunity. It fits the product and has an Easy rating, but its estimated demand is only 10 searches/month. A useful booking page can cover related questions and the Google Calendar integration together; do not create a separate page for every wording variation.

Use **AI receptionist for small business** as a longer-term target for a dedicated SimplAssist Voice page. It has much more demand (2,900 estimated searches/month) but Ahrefs rates it Hard. Keep the approachable SimplAssist Voice branding while naturally explaining the established category term and Full Suite availability. Avoid implying voice is included for $10.

## Method and limitations

- Scope: U.S. searches, English keyword phrases; checked September 17, 2026.
- Exact numeric estimates below were read directly from [SearchVolume.io](https://searchvolume.io/) after selecting United States and submitting 20 candidates. The tool describes volume as an average over the preceding 12 months, using its own tools and third-party purchased data. Its results did not expose the precise underlying dataset period or freshness for each keyword. These are approximate estimates, not directly verified Google counts.
- Ahrefs qualitative organic keyword difficulty and coarse volume buckets were read directly from the [free keyword generator](https://ahrefs.com/keyword-generator/), United States selected. No paid database was used. Easy, Medium, and Hard are the provider's labels, not guaranteed ranking outcomes. N/A means unavailable, not low competition.
- The tools disagree for some phrases: SearchVolume.io shows 70 for `ai website chatbot` and 90 for `ai chatbot for small business`; Ahrefs shows >100 for both. Preserve this uncertainty rather than blending figures into a false precise estimate.
- Sampled search results and competitor landing pages were reviewed for intent and positioning. No competitor's private traffic data was accessed. Search-result order was not treated as a stable Google ranking report.
- SimplAssist Search Console was not accessed during the initial keyword research. It was subsequently reviewed on the same date; see [the verified baseline](./search-console-baseline-2026-09-17.md) for actual queries, traffic, and indexing. Keyword-demand estimates below are separate from those site-specific measurements.
- Do not add overlapping keyword variants to claim a unique audience total. A reported zero can reflect insufficient data or rounding; it does not establish that nobody searches the phrase.

## Competitor observations

1. [Zoho SalesIQ](https://www.zoho.com/salesiq/chatbot/small-business.html) has a small-business chatbot page. [G2](https://learn.g2.com/chatbots-for-small-businesses) has a comparison guide covering established products. The small-business query has mixed product and comparison intent; Medium does not mean an uncontested result page.
2. [Social Intents](https://www.socialintents.com/ai-chatbot-for-website.html) and [Chat Data](https://www.chat-data.com/ai-chatbot-for-website) use dedicated AI-chatbot-for-website landing pages. Broad website chatbot phrases already have focused commercial competitors.
3. [Chatlyfy](https://chatlyfy.com/affordable-ai-chatbot) explicitly targets affordability and advertises paid single-bot plans from $7/month, subject to limits/add-ons. SimplAssist's $10 offer is accessible, but should not be described as the cheapest. Explain included features, limits, setup, and booking value.
4. [Smart Text Back](https://www.smarttextback.com/) targets missed-call recovery directly. Its clear trigger-and-outcome explanation illustrates the commercial intent: an unanswered call causes an automatic text response. Ahrefs Easy does not mean no competitors.
5. [BotPenguin](https://botpenguin.com/use-cases/appointment-bookings) has an appointment-booking use-case page, and [Small Business Chatbot](https://smallbusinesschatbot.com/integrations/google-calendar) has a dedicated Google Calendar integration page. Product demonstrations and specific integration explanations suit this intent. Do not copy competitors' unsupported performance claims or attribute their capabilities to SimplAssist.
6. [Open](https://www.open.cx/voice/for-small-business) has a small-business AI receptionist page. Voice is a real commercial category with existing focused competitors, consistent with the harder difficulty rating.

## Traffic scenarios, not forecasts

To illustrate scale, assume the site earns 3% or 10% of the estimated searches as organic clicks. These percentages are hypothetical, not measured SimplAssist CTRs or promised outcomes for a particular position.

| Keyword | Estimated searches/month | At assumed 3% click share | At assumed 10% click share |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| chatbot for small business | 140 | about 4 clicks | 14 clicks |
| missed call text back | 390 | about 12 clicks | 39 clicks |
| ai receptionist for small business | 2,900 | 87 clicks | 290 clicks |

Formula: estimated search demand × assumed share of searches generating a click to SimplAssist. These are clicks, not unique visitors or customers. With weak rankings the result may be near zero. Search features, intent, snippet appeal, device, and actual position affect outcomes. Related searches can add traffic, but the evidence here does not support promising hundreds of visits from the homepage's main exact phrase alone.

## AI search implications

[Google's official guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide) says foundational SEO remains relevant to AI Overviews and AI Mode. It emphasizes useful original content, clear structure, and technical eligibility, and does not require exact-match coverage of every phrase or special AI markup. Prioritize accurate explanations, visible pricing, actual demos, real examples, and substantive product evidence.

These Google-search keyword estimates do not measure ChatGPT prompt volume or predict AI citations. Visibility in Google, AI Overviews, or ChatGPT cannot be guaranteed by keyword edits. The proposed pages supply clear information worth retrieving, while subsequent measurement is needed to establish results.
